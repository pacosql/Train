                                   At a Kumon Centre, you’ll find no blackboards or
                                   lectures. Instead of passively receiving instruction
                                   from teachers or tutors, Kumon Students actively
                                   develop self-learning skills. Here’s how:

                                        Each student progresses at their own
                                        pace through an individualized lesson plan
                                        established by their Instructor.

                                        Students do daily assignments that take about
                                        30 minutes per subject, on paper worksheets or
                                        on a tablet via Kumon Connect.

                                        Students work under the supervision of Kumon
                                        Centre staff twice per week – in person or
                                        virtually – and independently the other days.

                                        Each worksheet teaches a lesson that’s one
                                        small step more advanced than the lesson
                                        before, so students progress smoothly and gain
                                        confidence with each new success.
